Privacy Policy Last updated: [09/072026] This Privacy Policy explains how 3D Live Wallpapers we handles information when you use our Android live wallpaper application. We built this app to be simple: it does not require an account, does not have a login system, and does not operate its own backend server. Most of what this policy covers relates to the third-party advertising services the app uses to show rewarded ads for unlocking optional visual effects. Information We Collect The app itself does not collect or transmit any personal information to us directly. Your settings within the app (such as your chosen frame rate, skybox, color grading, and unlocked effects) are stored only locally on your device and are never sent anywhere. However, the app displays ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), which may include mediation partners such as Vungle (Liftoff Monetize). These advertising SDKs automatically collect certain information in order to serve, measure, and prevent fraud on ads shown in the app, including: IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate (not precise) location Advertising identifiers, such as your Android Advertising ID and App Set ID App interaction data, such as ad views, taps, and video completions Diagnostic and performance data, such as app launch time and crash/performance information This data is collected and transmitted directly by these advertising SDKs to their own servers — it does not pass through, or get stored by, any server we operate, because we don't operate one. How This Information Is Used To display and target advertising within the app To measure ad performance To detect and prevent invalid traffic and ad fraud Third-Party Services The app uses the following third-party advertising services. Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — Google Privacy Policy Vungle / Liftoff Monetize — Liftoff Privacy Policy If you stop using one of these mediation partners, remove it from this list — the disclosures here should always match what your app actually does. Your Choices & Ad Personalization You can manage or reset your device's advertising identifier, and limit ad personalization, at any time through your device's own settings: On Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device/Android version) Or via Google Ads Settings Because the app has no account system, there is no in-app data deletion request feature. Resetting your Advertising ID, as described above, is the primary way to limit data associated with your device for advertising purposes. Data Security All data collected by the advertising SDKs used in this app is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). Children's Privacy This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.
